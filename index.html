About me

I’m Jennifer Baldwin, a student filmmaker based in Columbus, Ohio, studying Moving Image Production with a minor in Theatre at The Ohio State University. I’m drawn to narrative work, especially horror, comedy, and animation.

I’m detail-oriented, and I care most about the planning that makes a shoot run smoothly: scripts, shot lists, and clear communication. I’m a member of the Mad Royal Film Society, where I collaborate with other students on short films.
